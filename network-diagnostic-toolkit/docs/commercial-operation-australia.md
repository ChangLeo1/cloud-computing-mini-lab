# Commercial Operation Notes (Australia)

This page is general operational guidance for using the toolkit as part of a small paid IT-support service. It is **not legal, tax or accounting advice**.

## ABN and business setup

If the activity is genuinely being carried on as a business, an Australian Business Number (ABN) is commonly used to identify the business for invoicing and tax/business dealings. Australian Government guidance notes that ABN registration is free for eligible businesses and that business clients may have withholding obligations when a supplier does not quote an ABN.

Official guidance:
- https://business.gov.au/registrations/register-for-an-australian-business-number-abn
- https://business.gov.au/registrations/register-for-taxes/tax-registration-for-your-business

## GST

The general GST registration threshold is GST turnover of AUD 75,000 or more for a business/enterprise (with special rules for some activities and entities). Registration can also be voluntary below the threshold in applicable cases. Check current ATO rules before invoicing GST.

Official guidance:
- https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/registering-for-gst

## Income and records

A sole trader generally reports business income and expenses in the individual tax return and should keep appropriate business records. Business structure and obligations depend on the actual circumstances.

Official guidance:
- https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/starting-registering-or-closing-a-business/starting-your-own-business/business-structures-key-tax-obligations

## Australian Consumer Law

When consumer guarantees apply to an IT-support service, service providers must use due care and skill; services may also need to be fit for a disclosed purpose in applicable circumstances and supplied within a reasonable time if no timeframe is agreed. Avoid unsupported claims such as "guaranteed to fix every network problem".

Official guidance:
- https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/buying-products-and-services/consumer-rights-and-guarantees

## Recommended client workflow

1. Confirm the customer and authorised representative.
2. Define assets, network scope and approved activities in writing.
3. Agree the price, deliverables and any exclusions before work starts.
4. Diagnose with the least intrusive checks first.
5. Obtain separate approval for disruptive configuration changes.
6. Produce a redacted copy if a case study will be used publicly.
7. Store client reports securely and delete/de-identify them when no longer required, subject to applicable legal/contractual obligations.
8. Keep invoice and business/tax records required for the chosen business structure.

Use `docs/service-scope-template.md` and `docs/authorisation-and-scope.md` as starting points.
